The follow-up study

SimulatedA group of cognitively normal adults aged 70 to 75 takes the short story test now, then again at 6 months, 1, 2, 5 and 10 years, each time with a new story. Some carry a gene (one copy of APOE e4) that raises the chance of memory decline from Alzheimer's. Who is diagnosed with MCI or dementia, and when, is drawn from published rates for each gene group (Qian et al. 2017), never from the test. Press Play to watch the study, and point at any person to see their scores.

Bars: 95% interval from one test. Dashed: the hidden truth, when shown.

Each row compares one group with people never diagnosed in the study. Group test: the model-based test of the gap (p below 0.05 means the groups' gaps differ). Gap difference: context minus content, in units of healthy people's spread. AUC: how often one ill person scores worse than one healthy person (0.5 is a coin toss, 1 is perfect). Change: each person against their own first test.
